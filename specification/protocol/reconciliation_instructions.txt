COLLABORATIVE RECONCILIATION PROCEDURE
Release 0.4.1. These instructions document the completed bounded procedure and
provide a reusable workflow. They do not create a new independent coding test.

1. Freeze independent outputs before comparison. Retain first-complete outputs,
   mechanical repairs, final annotations, configuration and access records,
   source packets, prompts, codebook, schema, checker, and their hashes.
2. Align propositions before comparing classifications. Include unanimous
   claims, unmatched coverage, and consequential qualifiers in source review.
   Record independent agreement before any discussion or correction.
3. Prepare an issue packet from complete relevant sources, evidence anchors,
   competing readings, and the frozen rules. State the exact decision object and
   downstream claim. Do not frame majority preference as the reference answer.
4. Obtain separate initial reviewer positions. Each identifies the supported
   interpretation, strongest alternative, source warrant, rule, and consequence
   of retaining uncertainty. Preserve and hash these positions before exchange.
5. Supply all initial positions for one bounded peer-response round. Each
   reviewer states what changed, why it changed, and what remains unresolved.
   Additional rounds require a specific unresolved evidence question; agreement
   alone is not the objective. In the completed study three fresh reviewers
   reviewed ten issues, then each supplied one response after reading peers.
6. The coordinator distinguishes execution errors, criterion ambiguity,
   defensible representations, and insufficient evidence. Preserve dissent and
   reasoned alternatives. A preferred convention can coexist with a bounded
   alternative when both support the same institutional conclusion.
7. Apply accepted corrections to new derived views. Log every changed JSON path,
   before and after value, decision identifier, rationale, and technical versus
   interpretive change. Never overwrite independent annotations or source text.
8. Check derived views, exact anchors, lineage, and replay of every correction.
   Compare conclusions against unresolved alternatives. Block claims whose
   identity, attribution, authority, implementation, or chronology remains
   unsupported; do not require every harmless representation difference to end.
9. Report independent agreement, corrections, and reconciled interpretations
   separately. Post-discussion consensus is an adjudication outcome. It is not
   an independent reliability score or proof that the common interpretation is
   correct. Same-provider contexts are not cross-model validation.

Human responsibility concerns research purpose, consequential interpretive
judgment, and choices among competing values. Routine full human recoding is
not a condition of this protocol. Escalate unresolved value choices explicitly;
do not let agreement among agents choose whose policy preferences should prevail.

Release control
Use release 0.4.1 instructions with schema_version 0.4. Preserve the evaluated
v0.4 checker unchanged. Future new-topic use starts with a prospective transfer
check under the frozen package. If the rules require substantive revision,
open a new version and identify affected outputs; do not silently adjust the
codebook during a claimed fixed-protocol evaluation.
