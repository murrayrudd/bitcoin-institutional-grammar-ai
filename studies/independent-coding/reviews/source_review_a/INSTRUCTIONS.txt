Do not receive condition names, agreement scores, prior reconciliation decisions, or the other reviewer's output. Version masking can be incomplete because schemas differ; record that limitation. This review is revisable AI judgment, not a reference truth set.

Inspect all retained units and all assigned entries for material omissions. Check every relation, recognition and disposition event, authority claim, documentary assessment, application claim, challenge, and outcome or mechanism clause. Review unanimous claims as carefully as disagreements. Do not use voting to establish correctness.

For each finding, supply exact source evidence, affected annotation IDs, the applicable rule, diagnosis, effect, and proposed resolution. Diagnosis is criterion_ambiguity, execution_failure, defensible_alternative, or unresolved. Effect is interpretation_changing, evidence_basis, convention_only, coverage_or_scope, or unresolved. Group a shared underlying error across annotations once while preserving all affected runs; do not count repeated quotations or repeated runs as independent findings.

Prioritize proposition identity, force and negation, material conditions, attribution, authorization, time, implementation, and higher governance claims. Separate an unsupported argument-role label from an altered institutional conclusion. Preserve alternatives when sources cannot decide. If the codebook itself is ambiguous, identify the missing distinction instead of inventing a retrospective mandatory criterion.

For documentary claims, compare the exact proposition and its date. For recognition, distinguish ordinary assent, maintained personal commitment, interpersonal recognition, and repeat support across distinct episodes. For dispute, identify the challenged content, authority, application, or premise and verify a common object. For technical consequences, distinguish reported action, observed artifact, integration, and network adoption.
